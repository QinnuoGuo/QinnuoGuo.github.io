This project studies the modeling of **spatial motion patterns in non-stationary environments**.

The framework combines event-triggered representations with probabilistic and learning-based models to capture changing motion dynamics across space and time.

The work was published at **IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2025**.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS60139.2025.11247709)
