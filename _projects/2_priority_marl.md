This project studies **joint control and communication in decentralized multi-agent systems**.

Instead of treating communication as an independent discrete decision, the method learns a continuous **communication priority**, allowing agents with the highest priorities to communicate when bandwidth is limited.

The work was accepted to the **23rd IFAC World Congress 2026**.
