My current PhD research focuses on **safe and adaptive robot learning for parallel robotic systems**, with an emphasis on physical human–robot interaction.

I am particularly interested in combining **reinforcement learning, world models, impedance control, and control-theoretic safety methods** to enable robots to operate safely and compliantly in uncertain and contact-rich environments.

This project is ongoing at **Université Laval**.
