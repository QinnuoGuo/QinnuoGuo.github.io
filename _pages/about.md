I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at **Université Laval**, supervised by
[Prof. Hanqing Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=uXifdx0AAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and [Prof. Clément Gosselin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=O9VSIMoAAAAJ&hl=en).
My research focuses on **parallel robots, safe and stable reinforcement learning, world models, and physical human–robot interaction**.

I am particularly interested in developing learning-based robotic systems that can
interact safely and compliantly with humans and complex environments.

Before joining Université Laval, I received my M.Sc. in **Control, Robotics and Autonomous
Systems** from **Aalto University**, where I worked on learning-based event-triggered control,
multi-agent reinforcement learning, and communication-efficient decision making.

### Research Interests

- Robotics
- World Models
- Reinforcement Learning
- Safe and Stable Control
- Physical Human–Robot Interaction
- Multi-Agent Systems
- Parallel and Compliant Robotics
